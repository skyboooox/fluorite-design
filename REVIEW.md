# Delivery checklist

Verify affected pages and every use of shared components. For documentation-only changes, check links and generated output.

- [ ] Navigation, entry points, back, cancel, confirmation and deletion work correctly.
- [ ] Light/dark themes, tokens, typography, icons, dimensions and spacing are consistent.
- [ ] Primary and secondary actions are clear; control states and industrial decoration follow meaning.
- [ ] Filters preserve selection; counts, order, drafts and previews are correct.
- [ ] Previews perform no business writes; requests prevent duplicates, recover from failure and report actual results.
- [ ] Scrolling clips correctly; fixed bars do not obscure content; long text, translations and complex values do not overflow.
- [ ] Loading, missing, offline, stale, unauthorized and error states are accurate.
- [ ] Chart density, units, time, extrema and disconnection gaps are correct.
- [ ] Target viewports, font scaling, mouse, touch and keyboard remain usable.
- [ ] Focus, names, semantics, contrast and reduced motion meet requirements.
- [ ] Figma, Web, guidelines, screenshots and downloads agree.

Report validation scope, results and untested items. Distinguish simulation, readback and physical effects.
